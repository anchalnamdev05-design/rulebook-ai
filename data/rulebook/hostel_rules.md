# University Rulebook

## 9.2 Hostel Leave Permission
Residents seeking overnight leave must submit an online request to the hostel warden at least 24 hours before departure. A resident may take hostel leave for up to seven consecutive days with warden approval; longer leave requires the Chief Warden's written approval. The request records destination, dates, emergency contact, and expected return time. The warden may refuse a request during a safety restriction, examination movement, or disciplinary measure and must record the reason. On return, residents sign the arrival register. Repeated late returns may lead to a residence conduct review, but a single delay caused by a documented transport disruption is considered by the warden.

## 9.7 Hostel Absence Limit
No hostel resident may remain away from the residence for more than five consecutive days during a teaching term without prior approval from the Chief Warden. Requests made only to a hostel warden do not authorize an absence beyond five days. The resident must include travel details and a reachable emergency contact. The stricter approval is recorded before departure where possible. Emergency absence should be reported as soon as practicable and supporting information supplied on return.

## 9.5 Identity Cards and Guests
A university identity card is personal and must be produced by its holder when requested by residence staff. Guests may enter only during published visiting hours after being signed in by their host. Another student may not use a resident's identity card. Residents are responsible for room security, keys, and the behavior of signed-in guests. Staff may record an incident and refer repeated breaches to the residence conduct process.

Residents should retain approved leave notices and arrival records. A residence permission concerns the dates and person named in it and cannot be transferred by informal agreement.

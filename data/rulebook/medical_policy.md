# University Rulebook — Medical Policy

## 7.1 — Medical Documentation

A student whose health affects attendance or assessment should contact the University Medical Officer or Student Services through the secure process. Medical documents should be dated, identify the period affected, and describe functional restrictions relevant to study or assessment. A diagnosis alone does not automatically change an academic requirement. The designated professional decides whether the evidence supports a recommendation and records the scope of any recommendation. Students should not send sensitive records through a public class channel or ask an instructor to make a medical determination.

The medical office may ask for clarification, a further examination, or a report from a registered treating professional. It may recommend attendance support, make-up practical work, adjusted assessment arrangements, or academic leave according to the applicable procedure. A recommendation is not an approval from the examination authority. The student receives a written decision or referral identifying the course, period, and conditions. Students should keep the approval and submit it to affected offices by the stated deadline.

## 7.2 — Approved Medical Attendance Exemption

A student with an approved medical exemption may be permitted to appear for a final examination with attendance as low as 60% in the affected course. The exemption requires dated medical records, a recommendation from the University Medical Officer, and approval recorded before the examination timetable is published. The approval applies only to the named course and period. It does not automatically excuse missed internal assessments, laboratory safety requirements, or attendance in another course.

A diagnosis, prescription, or informal message does not itself create the exemption. The student should submit the request as soon as the health circumstance is known and respond to any request for additional evidence. The approving authority records the eligible percentage or other condition and communicates the decision through the official channel. If the request is refused or incomplete, the ordinary attendance requirement remains applicable unless another written decision changes it. The student should contact the examination office when the approval and the published eligibility list appear inconsistent.

## 7.3 — Medical Examination Absence

A student absent from a scheduled examination because of a documented medical emergency may apply to the Controller of Examinations within five working days. The application includes the examination name, date, student identity number, hospital certificate, and contact details. A review panel considers whether the evidence covers the examination time and whether the application was submitted through the required channel. If approved, the student receives an examination-absence decision and information about the next available assessment opportunity.

This provision addresses documented medical emergencies. It does not state an outcome for absence caused by a family wedding, vacation, ordinary travel, social event, or an avoidable timetable choice. Students should not treat a medical absence procedure as a general permission to miss an examination. Notification alone does not guarantee a second sitting. The decision is recorded by the Controller and may be reviewed only through the route described in the decision notice.

## 7.4 — Health Support and Return to Study

A student returning after a significant health absence may ask Student Services for a support meeting. The meeting can coordinate academic communication, accessibility arrangements, welfare referral, and a realistic return plan. It does not erase absences or alter a grade without a decision from the responsible academic authority. A return plan may include a reduced course load, staged laboratory participation, or referral to a professional service where allowed by programme requirements.

Confidential information is shared only with offices that need it for support, safety, assessment arrangements, or fair administration. Instructors receive the practical adjustment notice rather than unnecessary clinical detail. Students should report if an approved adjustment cannot be implemented. A support meeting cannot promise that an examination attempt will be reopened, that a fee will be waived, or that progression will be approved; those matters use the published academic, finance, and progression procedures.

# University Rulebook

## 8.1 Merit Scholarship Eligibility
Merit scholarships are considered annually for full-time students with a cumulative GPA of at least 8.0 and no active disciplinary sanction. Applications must be submitted by 30 September with an official transcript and faculty recommendation. The scholarship panel considers academic performance, satisfactory course completion, leadership, and the availability of awards. A student on an approved leave may apply if the panel can verify the prior academic year. Awards are conditional on continued enrollment and satisfactory conduct. The Finance Office publishes the payment schedule after selections are approved.

## 8.4 Scholarship Continuation
A recipient must remain enrolled at the load stated in the award letter and meet any renewal GPA. The panel may pause an award while a disciplinary or enrollment matter is decided. A pause is not a finding of misconduct. If eligibility is restored, the Finance Office explains whether missed installments can be paid under the award terms. A scholarship does not waive tuition, examination, residence, or laboratory charges unless the award letter expressly says so. Students must report a change in enrollment load, course withdrawal, or disciplinary status to the scholarship office.

## 5.8 Academic Progression
To progress to the next academic year, a student must earn at least 70 percent of the credits prescribed for the current year and may carry no more than two failed courses. The Academic Progress Committee reviews borderline cases after final results are published. The committee records the credit calculation and gives the student a written outcome. An approved concession is limited to the circumstance named in the decision and cannot be treated as a general waiver. Students may appeal a progression decision within ten working days when a published appeal ground exists.

A student should read the current award letter and progression notice together with the handbook. Portal publication supplies the operative date for deadlines. Informal assurances do not change a published eligibility threshold.
